Travel tips

  • Book flights and hotels early, prices usually rise close to the date.
  • Keep copies of your documents separate from the originals.
  • Pack light and bring clothes you can layer.
  • Get travel insurance, especially for longer trips.
  • Download offline maps and save your tickets on your phone.
